How We Protect Your Privacy

Client-Side Processing

All image conversion happens entirely within your web browser using JavaScript. Your images are processed locally on your device and never sent to any server.

No Data Collection

We don't collect, store, or analyze any of your personal images or data. We have no database, no user accounts, and no tracking systems.

No Registration Required

Use our Wplace Paint Tool without creating accounts, providing email addresses, or sharing any personal information. Anonymous usage is our default.

Open Source Transparency

Our code is transparent and can be inspected. You can verify that we do exactly what we claim - local processing with no data transmission.

Information We Do NOT Collect

To be completely transparent, here's what we specifically do NOT collect:

  • Your uploaded images or generated pixel art
  • Personal information (name, email, address, phone)
  • Device information or browser fingerprints
  • Location or IP address data
  • Usage patterns or behavior analytics
  • Cookies for tracking purposes
  • Account information (we have no user accounts)
  • Payment information (our service is completely free)

Limited Technical Information

The only information we might collect is basic technical data necessary to operate the website:

  • Server logs for basic website functionality (not linked to users)
  • Error logs to fix technical issues (anonymous)
  • Basic traffic statistics (anonymous visitor counts)

Important: Even this minimal technical data is not connected to your images, your identity, or your specific usage patterns.

Local Storage

We may use your browser's local storage to:

  • Remember your language preference
  • Save your Wplace Paint Tool settings (pixel size, scaling method)
  • Store temporary processing data during conversion

Note: Local storage data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. You can clear this data anytime through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses minimal third-party services:

  • Tailwind CSS CDN: For styling (no tracking)
  • Web hosting service: To serve the website files

These services may have their own privacy policies, but they cannot access your images or personal data since everything is processed locally.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, most traditional privacy rights don't apply. However:

  • You can stop using the service anytime
  • You can clear local storage data through your browser
  • You can request information about our technical practices
  • You can report privacy concerns to us

Security

Since your images never leave your device, they're as secure as your own computer. We use HTTPS to protect the website itself, but your actual image data never travels over the internet. This makes our approach inherently more secure than cloud-based image processors.

Children's Privacy

Our service is safe for children because we don't collect any personal information. Parents can feel confident that their children's privacy is protected when using our Wplace Paint Tool.

Changes to Privacy Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we'll update this policy and clearly mark the changes. Since we don't collect contact information, we can't notify users directly, so please check this page occasionally for updates.
